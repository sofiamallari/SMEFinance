## Requirement gaps and conflicts

**Implemented without confirmed business requirement:** matching catalog/weights, readiness factors, stage gates, RBF commercial and qualification parameters, incentive rules, MDR/taxes, forecast/alert calibration. Later accounting/import/reconciliation/refund/role/consent rules also need source approval. Tests show intended behavior, not authorization.

**Original requirements missing:** cannot assert omissions from an unavailable original specification. Observable specification gaps include five-step completion criteria, sustained graduation, lender-calibrated eligibility, explicit recipient choice and application review lifecycle. Reward text does not establish implemented real cashback, subsidies or lender ceiling/collateral changes.

**Changes from earliest available prototype:** Task02 added canonical records/import/DQ/staff grants; Task03 added shared financial projection, refunds/failure states, verified claims and confirmed actions. RBF added defensive checks while commercial formulas/config remain. Local journey adds honest unassessed statuses, not completion thresholds.

**Cross-module conflicts:**

1. Matching gates eligibility but quote/packet creation does not inherently enforce it or displayed minimum.
2. External digital activity qualifies RBF digital share while sizing/repayment use ecosystem rails. Synthetic/manual/imported labels are not bank verification.
3. Stage6 prerequisite disagrees with config comment; numeric succession movement counts as graduation and changes product exclusions.
4. Reviews append while application stays submitted; multiple consents do not provide explicit business recipient selection.
5. Revenue is not wallet cash; receivables/historical inflows may overlap without accounting semantics; buffer excludes scheduled payables.
6. Device access affects readiness despite stated inclusion principle.
7. Extrapolated growth can qualify milestone text without implemented real rewards.

## Summary and demo recommendation

All eleven modules reviewed; no independently confirmed A or proven C rules. Most financial rules are B. Later additions can be dated but original policy/authorship is D. Technical safeguards are E, not evidence of unauthorized business policy.

Treat pricing, eligibility, scores/stages, incentives, tax values, repayment/collection forecasts, wallet balances and settlement as provisional/simulated during the demo. Clearly label synthetic/imported/simulated provenance; valid input is not verified settlement, quote is not approval and sandbox review is not underwriting. Keep safeguards active. Five-step completion remains unassessed pending approved criteria.

## Five highest-priority business decisions

1. Approve one financing eligibility/ranking specification: catalog, weights, affordability, verified sources and enforcement at quote/submission.
2. Approve RBF commercial/model terms: history, sales basis, limits, fees/discounts, remittance, term thresholds and annualized-cost wording.
3. Approve readiness/stages/graduation: factors/gates, Stage6 exception, sustained progression and five-step completion evidence.
4. Approve payment/accounting/incentives: gross/net/tax/refund semantics, reconciliation tolerance, settlement and reward qualification/funding.
5. Approve application/consent governance: recipient choice, pre-submission Agent access, review/status transitions, expiry/revocation and recovery.
