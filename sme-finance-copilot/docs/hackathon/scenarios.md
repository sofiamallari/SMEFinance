# Six CNY merchant demonstration scenarios

These fictional businesses retain original names, IDs, sectors/countries and relative scenario patterns while receiving freshly generated RMB budgets. Country labels are not proof of Chinese registration. Fixed seed history ends 2026-09-30; selected recent canonical/sandbox examples are separately identified.

| ID / merchant | Scenario | Fresh starting daily sales assumption (CNY) |
|---|---|---:|
| M001 — Lola Nena’s Kakanin Stall | Informal micro-stall, closures, low capture and cash pressure | 680 |
| M006 — Bayanihan Sari-Sari Store | Neighborhood retail and gradual digital adoption | 1,850 |
| M002 — Tani Coffee & Tofu House | Growth, high utilization and late customer receivables; routed demo applications | 3,600 |
| M003 — Baan Rattan Crafts | Production capacity, confirmed orders and financing readiness | 8,200 |
| M004 — Sen Lotus Bakery Group | Multi-unit business and expansion | 26,500 |
| M005 — Warisan Batik House | Mature retail and succession flag | 11,800 |

These are generation anchors, not current dashboard totals, measured averages or FX conversions. Do not force a maturity stage or financing eligibility to match an old screenshot. The actual scores and recommendations are recomputed by current services.

Seeded roles are demo_admin, demo_owner, demo_staff and demo_bank. Memberships are scoped by the synthetic manifest; Staff initially has no operational grants. Current demo consent structure covers two merchants and two submitted applications are routed for M002, with append-only simulated review examples. Inspect actual current state before a live presentation; later user actions can change counts/status.
