# End-to-end demonstration journey

Use an initialized isolated synthetic dataset and privately supplied credentials. No step authorizes real banking activity.

1. **System Admin:** inspect Merchant Management and User Management. Show that administration does not reveal financial records. Confirm Owner/Staff/Bank assignments without adding unnecessary rights.
2. **Owner:** select M002 and review Business Overview CNY figures and source dates. Open Sales & Payments, Business Growth and Alerts; distinguish historical report filters from on-demand assessments.
3. **Owner delegation:** grant only the required operations to existing Staff for the selected merchant. Staff signs in again, sees delegated modules and remains unable to submit applications or grant Bank consent.
4. **Transactions:** record a distinct synthetic transaction or use the fresh CNY examples. For imports select sources/worksheets, map, validate, inspect actionable findings, preview signed totals and confirm only the intended batch. Demonstrate duplicate or invalid data using a disposable batch; cancel it when finished.
5. **Payments:** create and explicitly confirm one distinct sandbox collection, simulate payment and inspect fee/settlement. Do not duplicate the imported sale as another collection.
6. **Analysis:** Owner reviews updated history, quality, dashboard and cash-flow forecast. Ask an authorized AI question and show evidence/limitations; do not promise a live Gemini response unless separately verified.
7. **Stress Test:** if enabled, review baseline, adjust assumptions, explicitly run and compare the chart. Show stale-result labeling after changing controls, then inspect details. This never writes financial transactions.
8. **Financing:** explore indicative matching/RBF. Save an RBF draft, explicitly select the active Bank and grant the required applications authorization plus any chosen financial categories. Review the exact proposal and confirm submission.
9. **Bank:** open its routed request and consent-limited Financial Review. Record one supported simulated outcome with notes; inspect appended history. Explain that this is not approval/disbursement.
10. **Owner:** refresh Applications and read Bank feedback. Revoke a grant if intentionally demonstrating access changes; future protected Bank reads stop while routing metadata can remain. Prior downloads cannot be recalled.

Before presentation, verify dates, credentials, memberships, consent expiry, feature flag and available data. Do not weaken permissions or change fixed formulas to make the demo pass. [Role guides](../user-guide/README.md) · [evidence scope](evidence.md).
