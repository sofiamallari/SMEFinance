# Dashboard and Business Overview

Current implemented guide · 15 October 2026.

## Purpose

Review recorded performance and navigate to maturity and business alerts.

## Access and prerequisites

Owner or Staff with the required delegated Dashboard/assessment/forecast reads.

## Navigation

Dashboard → Overview / Sales & Payments / Business Growth / Alerts.

## Step-by-step instructions

1. Select the merchant.
2. On Overview, choose Start, End and Granularity, then Apply.
3. Review Recorded Net Sales, Expenses, Net Income and Digital Capture.
4. Use Sales & Payments for the two mix charts and transaction/revenue trends; inspect actual period/currency labels.
5. Use Business Growth for maturity and Alerts for available business alerts.
6. Expand Data Sources & Freshness where available; review synthetic-data and coverage disclosures.

## Understanding results and statuses

Financial filters affect the historical report. They do not imply a fresh maturity or continuous alert calculation. Transaction counts, recorded channel value, operating estimates and cash balances have different meanings.

## Common errors and troubleshooting

Check the reporting period, selected business, transaction status and data provenance when totals differ. Empty results do not prove zero actual business activity.

## Important limitations

Recorded Net Income is the existing report metric, not audited taxable profit. The current Revenue Split caption identifies recorded channel values; do not reinterpret it as bank settlement or unavailable gross source evidence.
