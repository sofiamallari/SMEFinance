# Dashboard and Business Overview

Current implemented guide · 15 October 2026.

## Purpose

Review recorded performance, cash flow and hypothetical operating scenarios.

## Access and prerequisites

Owner or Staff with the required delegated Dashboard/assessment/forecast reads.

## Navigation

Dashboard -> Overview / Cash Flow / Survival Simulation. The header bell opens existing business alerts.

## Step-by-step instructions

1. Select the merchant.
2. On Overview, choose Start, End and Granularity, then Apply.
3. Review Recorded Net Sales, Expenses, Net Income and Digital Capture.
4. Review all four charts and Business Highlights on Overview; inspect actual period/currency labels.
5. Use Cash Flow for the existing forecast and Survival Simulation for the existing Owner-only, feature-flagged operating scenario.
6. Open the header bell for on-demand business alerts; no unread state is tracked.
7. Above the reporting filters, select Export overview as PDF, then Save as PDF in the browser print dialog. The A4 report includes the displayed merchant, applied reporting period, summary, four charts and highlights. Allow the report window if blocked. The adjacent question-mark control explains Save as PDF.
8. Review synthetic-data and coverage disclosures. A compact maturity summary remains in Financing Overview.

## Understanding results and statuses

The two mix charts share a desktop row; each trend chart has its own full-width row. A partial-month note is derived from the applied monthly date range, without projecting missing transactions or changing totals. Financial filters affect the historical report. They do not imply a fresh maturity or continuous alert calculation. Transaction counts, recorded channel value, operating estimates and cash balances have different meanings.

## Common errors and troubleshooting

Check the reporting period, selected business, transaction status and data provenance when totals differ. Empty results do not prove zero actual business activity.

## Important limitations

PDF export uses browser printing rather than a direct binary download; rendering/page-number support depends on the browser. Recorded Net Income is the existing report metric, not audited taxable profit. The current Revenue Split caption identifies recorded channel values; do not reinterpret it as bank settlement or unavailable gross source evidence.
