# Financing and Revenue-Based Financing

Current implemented guide · 15 October 2026.

## Purpose

Explore indicative financing and prepare an explicitly confirmed request.

## Access and prerequisites

Owner; Staff may use delegated reads and own drafts but cannot submit. An assigned active Bank and the required applications authorization are needed for submission.

## Navigation

Financing → Overview / RBF Calculator / Applications.

## Step-by-step instructions

1. Review matching options and the compact business-readiness summary in Overview.
2. Open RBF Calculator and inspect eligibility, limits and the supported advance amount.
3. Review fee, total repayable, remittance percentage and estimated payoff examples.
4. In Applications choose New Application, enter supported RBF amount/Bank and Save Draft.
5. Owner reviews the draft and required Bank authorization, then prepares submission.
6. Review the exact proposal and select Confirm and Submit; read the resulting status and Bank feedback.

## Understanding results and statuses

Draft is not submitted. SUBMITTED_FOR_LENDER_REVIEW means recorded sandbox routing, not a financing decision. Calculator output is indicative and history-based.

## Common errors and troubleshooting

A missing active assigned Bank, missing applications-category authorization, stale proposal, unsupported amount or changed financial data can prevent submission. Recheck current state before retrying an uncertain operation.

## Important limitations

No separate Progression Engine/Financing Journey menu is part of current Financing navigation. No actual loan approval, rejection, remittance deduction or disbursement occurs.
