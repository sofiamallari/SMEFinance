# AI Financial Partner

Current implemented guide · 15 October 2026.

## Purpose

Ask about authorized business data and how to use the application.

## Access and prerequisites

Owner or delegated Staff with Copilot access and each required underlying read permission.

## Navigation

Header AI icon → existing AI Financial Partner drawer → AI Chat.

## Step-by-step instructions

1. Confirm the selected merchant.
2. Choose a suggested question or enter a question; Enter sends and Shift+Enter adds a line.
3. Ask about recorded performance, supported financing, cash flow, applications or application workflows.
4. Check the reporting period, source and limitations in the response.
5. Navigate modules without creating a second assistant; close/reopen the existing drawer as needed.
6. Review sensitive actions only through their existing authorized confirmation workflow.

## Understanding results and statuses

Tool access and claim verification are enforced server-side. Unavailable data is reported rather than inferred. A deterministic help or financial response is not proof of Gemini connectivity.

## Common errors and troubleshooting

Provider timeout/unavailability may produce a fallback or readable failure. A foreign-currency request does not authorize conversion. Switch merchant explicitly rather than asking the assistant to bypass scope.

## Important limitations

No credit approvals, real money transfers or permission overrides. Conversation persistence is session/page-context limited; refresh/logout restoration is not a promised cross-session history service. Bank portal has no AI drawer.
