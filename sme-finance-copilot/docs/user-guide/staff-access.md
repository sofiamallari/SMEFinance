# Staff Access and Delegation

Current implemented guide · 15 October 2026.

## Purpose

Delegate limited actions to Staff already assigned to a business.

## Access and prerequisites

Owner with merchant-staff management permission; System Admin handles account and membership provisioning.

## Navigation

Owner sidebar → Own-business User Management (`/owner/users`).

## Step-by-step instructions

1. Select the merchant and inspect its assigned Staff.
2. Review the intended tasks and current permission selections.
3. Choose only necessary grants, such as encoding, upload, history or reports.
4. Save the merchant-specific changes.
5. Ask the Staff member to sign in again and verify permitted modules.

## Understanding results and statuses

Different merchants have independent grants. Role-level security policy still bounds every selection. Saving delegation revokes the affected Staff sessions.

## Common errors and troubleshooting

If the account is not listed, ask System Admin to check its role/status and merchant membership. Missing module access is not fixed by sharing the Owner password.

## Important limitations

Delegation cannot grant Owner-only submission, Bank consent, platform policy administration or further delegation. This page is not unrestricted account creation.
