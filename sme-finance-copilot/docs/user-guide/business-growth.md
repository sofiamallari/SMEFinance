# Business Growth and Progression

Current implemented guide · 15 October 2026.

## Purpose

Understand the existing six-stage business maturity assessment.

## Access and prerequisites

Owner or Staff with assessment permission; financing summaries also require their appropriate reads.

## Navigation

Dashboard → Business Growth; a compact readiness summary also appears in Financing Overview.

## Step-by-step instructions

1. Select the merchant and open Business Growth.
2. Review the actual current stage and composite score.
3. Inspect Financial Health, Formalization, Digital Maturity, Market Access, Operational Capacity, Credit Readiness and Sustainability.
4. Review the next-stage gap, required gates and recommended improvement priority.
5. Expand Assessment Details; use the existing refresh control to request a new assessment when needed.

## Understanding results and statuses

The six backend labels are Survival Borrower, Striving Amateur, Steady Upgrader, Opportunity Seizer, Expanding Proprietor and Heir Seeker. Scores, gates and recommendations come from one assessment service.

## Common errors and troubleshooting

Scores can differ from an old screenshot because source data, dates or earlier implementations differ. Use the current selected merchant’s assessment rather than copying old scores.

## Important limitations

Maturity is not credit eligibility, journey completion or approval. The separate Identify → Profile → Place → Activate → Measure backend journey is preserved but removed from normal Owner navigation. No Activation Plan is shown in this view.
