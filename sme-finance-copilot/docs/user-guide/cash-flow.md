# Cash Flow

Current implemented guide · 15 October 2026.

## Purpose

Explore the existing projected balance and cash-related alerts.

## Access and prerequisites

Owner or Staff with delegated forecast access.

## Navigation

Dashboard -> Cash Flow. `/cash-flow` redirects authorized Dashboard users to that tab; it remains a standalone authorized view for accounts without Dashboard access.

## Step-by-step instructions

1. Select the merchant and open Cash Flow.
2. Read opening balance, lowest projected balance and cash-buffer indicators.
3. Inspect the forecast method and 30-day graph.
4. Review overdue-invoice, low-buffer or other alerts actually returned.
5. Return to records and obligations to investigate the underlying business issue.

## Understanding results and statuses

This forecast uses historical weekday patterns and scheduled items with the configured synthetic opening wallet. It is different from recorded net sales, income and the Survival operating-result graph.

## Common errors and troubleshooting

Check source dates, unpaid receivables/payables, debt-service assumptions and data completeness before interpreting a warning or a reassuring empty state.

## Important limitations

It is not a connected bank-account statement or guaranteed future balance. Old synthetic activity and missing obligations can limit usefulness.
